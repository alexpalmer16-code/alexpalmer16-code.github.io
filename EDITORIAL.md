# Editorial quality gate

The audience is sales and GTM leaders deciding how AI should change commercial work.
The aim is useful judgment, not a volume target. AI supports Alex's thinking; it
must not invent his experience, evidence or personality.

Use [WRITING.md](WRITING.md) for the voice, zero-em-dash rule and sentence-pattern
review. [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) tells repository-based agents which guidance to read.

## Classify each piece on two dimensions

Choose one primary subject and one format. Subjects describe what the piece is
about; formats describe how it earns its argument.

| Subject | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| AI-native commercial systems | Account intelligence, meeting preparation, pipeline inspection |
| Commercial judgment | Human control, quality, customer trust, tooling versus process |
| Leadership and adoption | Behaviour change, coaching, roles and operating rhythms |
| Emerging GTM shifts | Agents, changing economics, technology and skills |

| Format | Required substance |
| --- | --- |
| Analysis | A defensible thesis, evidence, counterargument and commercial decision |
| Playbook | Inputs, repeatable steps, human checks, output and failure cases |
| Experiment | What was tested, method, baseline, actual results and limitations |
| Prediction | A time horizon, reasoning, disconfirming evidence and revisit date |

Practical experiments run across the subjects rather than forming a duplicate
subject bucket. Over roughly ten pieces, aim for 40% analysis, 30% playbooks,
20% experiments and 10% responses to important developments. A response can be
labelled Analysis or Prediction; this is portfolio guidance, not a weekly quota.

## Input contract: private notes only

Before drafting, capture:

- The observation or experiment, and what actually happened.
- The target reader and decision they face.
- The thesis and conventional view being challenged.
- Firsthand evidence, source links and what remains unverified.
- A tradeoff, limitation or plausible counterargument.
- The decision Alex would make and the point he is prepared to defend.

For voice capture, ask one question at a time. Start with: “What happened this
week that changed how you think about commercial work?” Follow the answer,
challenge gaps, then read back the thesis for approval before drafting.
Distinguish missing evidence from a missing opinion. Do not fill either in.

## Before an article enters this public repository

- [ ] Alex has approved the text for public review. A branch is public too.
- [ ] There is a specific thesis, target reader and commercial implication.
- [ ] At least one meaningful decision, tradeoff, prediction or observation is Alex's own.
- [ ] No invented first-person stories, quotations, metrics, customer details or results.
- [ ] Customer, employer and personal information is removed or cleared for disclosure.
- [ ] Factual claims have appropriate linked sources; sources were read and actually support the claim.
- [ ] Firsthand observation, external evidence, inference and prediction are distinguishable.
- [ ] Missing research is recorded privately as a gap, not hidden by confident prose.
- [ ] The strongest counterargument and relevant limitations are addressed.
- [ ] The selected format meets its requirements above.
- [ ] Language is direct and commercially specific; remove hype, filler and generic AI conclusions.
- [ ] The final edit in WRITING.md is complete, including zero em dashes and a human review of repetitive sentence patterns.
- [ ] Worked examples distinguish intended outcomes from measured results; an illustrative workflow is labelled as such.
- [ ] A diagram is included only if it clarifies the argument; decorative art is optional.
- [ ] The ending gives a useful decision or question, not an automatic sales pitch.

## Before publishing

- [ ] The title, description, format, subject, date and reading estimate agree across the page, cards and sharing metadata.
- [ ] Reading time is a rounded estimate of body words / 200, minimum one minute.
- [ ] Sources and useful internal links work; no invented links to upcoming articles.
- [ ] All template tokens are replaced, and HTML special characters are escaped where needed.
- [ ] Local checks pass and the visual review in [PUBLISHING.md](PUBLISHING.md) is complete.
- [ ] Alex explicitly approves the exact revision to publish. New edits need a fresh review.

The automated checker catches em dashes in public HTML, the article template and
the project writing guides, including encoded HTML forms. It does not verify
personal authenticity, evidence quality or sentence-level judgment.

## Weekly production session: approximately one hour

| Minutes | Work |
| --- | --- |
| 0–10 | Voice interview: observation, thesis, decision and evidence |
| 10–25 | AI challenges the argument, flags gaps and structures a draft |
| 25–45 | Alex rewrites and approves the voice, evidence and conclusion |
| 45–55 | Prepare HTML, metadata and channel drafts; inspect the preview |
| 55–60 | Approve the revision, publish and record the next question |

This assumes the evidence already exists. Research, experiments and unresolved
quality issues take additional time; do not compress them to meet a timer.
Reminders are on hold. Distribution can be manual; no paid X integration is required.

## Content packet and feedback loop

Keep the private packet beside the source notes, outside this repository:
source brief → approved website article → LinkedIn perspective → X thread and
standalone posts → optional newsletter → metadata, useful diagrams and internal
links → objections, conversations and the next experiment.

Adapt each channel around its reader and format; do not publish every derivative
automatically. The website holds the full argument. LinkedIn develops its
professional implication. X tests individual ideas. Newsletter output is optional,
not a reason to build a newsletter system now.

For feedback, record the article URL, date, channel, response link, primary category,
short evidence excerpt and next action. Use: substantive agreement; objection;
practical question; reported use/result; commercial conversation; or low-signal.
Classification is provisional and needs the response text, not just a like count.
Track changes to the thesis and useful conversations alongside reach. Automation
and connected analytics access are not configured by this document.
