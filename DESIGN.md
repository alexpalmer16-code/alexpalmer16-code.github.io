# Alex Palmer — Website Design Direction

## Purpose

A personal research and publishing site about Sales, GTM and AI.

The site should position Alex as a commercially grounded operator exploring how AI changes revenue teams, commercial judgment and GTM systems.

## Visual direction

- Editorial rather than startup landing page
- Intelligent, restrained and commercially credible
- Warm off-white background
- Charcoal typography
- Dark green accent colour
- Strong typography and generous spacing
- Minimal navigation
- Limited animation
- Designed for comfortable long-form reading

## Avoid

- Neon AI gradients
- Robots, glowing brains or generic AI imagery
- Excessive animations
- SaaS-style feature grids
- Overly rounded cards
- Generic stock photography
- Overcrowded navigation

## Page principles

- Every page should have one clear purpose
- Writing should remain the visual focus
- Mobile reading must be excellent
- Calls to action should be subtle
- Use diagrams only when they clarify a real system
- Keep page load times fast

## Voice

- Commercially grounded
- Direct and natural
- Curious without being breathless about AI
- Specific about decisions and tradeoffs
- Clear about what is proven versus speculative
